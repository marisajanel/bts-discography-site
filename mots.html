Map of the Soul

'Persona' released April 12, 2019

'7' released February 21, 2020

'Map of the Soul: Persona' offered a profound exploration of identity and introspection. It was a pivotal release, setting the stage for BTS's subsequent evolution. The album seamlessly blends diverse musical styles while delving into themes of self-discovery and personal growth. The follow up, 'Map of the Soul: 7' expanded upon these themes, diving deeper into the complexities of the human psyche. Through intricate lyricism and diverse musical arrangements, BTS invites listeners on a reflective journey, confronting both the light and shadow within themselves.
