Young Forever released May 2, 2016

'The Most Beautiful Moment In Life' was released as a two-part series, which was later repackaged together as 'Young Forever.' The album represents moments in BTS's career and artistic journey. The theme of the albums consist of youth, love and the complexities of growing up in the modern world. 'The Most Beautiful Moment in Life' blends a variety of music styles, from hip-hop to pop-rock, showcasing BTS's versatility as artists. Tracks like 'I Need U,' 'Run,' and 'Fire' became anthems for a generation, resonating with listeners around the world with their heartfelt lyrics and infectious melodies. This album marked a turning point for BTS, propelling them to new heights of fame and cementing their status as global icons. Through their music, BTS explores the highs and lows of life, inspiring their fans to embrace their own journey and find beauty in every moment.
