Dark & Wild

Released August 19, 2014

'Dark & Wild' showcases BTS's evolution as artists and performers. This album represents a departure from their earlier sound, delving into darker themes and more mature subject matters. Tracks like 'Danger' and 'War of Hormone' exhibit BTS's signature blend of rap and vocal expertise, while songs like 'Let Me Know' and 'Rain' demonstrate their versatility and emotional depth. 'Dark & Wild' explores a wide range of genres from hip-hop to rock, and showcases BTS's ability to experiment with different musical styles. With it's raw energy, powerful yrics, and captivating performances, this album solidified BTS's position as trailblazers in the global music scene.
