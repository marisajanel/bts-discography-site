2 Cool 4 Skool

Released June 12, 2013

BTS's debut album. This album marked the beginning of their journey in the music industry. Released under Big Hit Entertainment, this album indroduced BTS's unique blend of hip-hop and pop elements. Its featured tracks like 'No More Dream' and 'We are Bulletproof Pt.2', showcased their powerful rap verses and dynamic choreography. '2 Cool 4 Skool' laid the foundation for BTS's future success, setting the stage for their rise to global prominence in the years to come.
