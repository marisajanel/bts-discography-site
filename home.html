About BTS

BTS, also known as Bangtan Boys, is a globally renowned South Korean boy band that has taken the music industry by storm with their performances and meaningful lyrics. Comprised of the members,

  • RM; Kim Namjoon
  • Jin; Kim Seok-jin
  • Suga; Min Yoongi
  • J-Hope; Jung Ho-seok
  • Jimin; Park Jimin
  • V; Kim Taehyung
  • Jungkook; Jeon Jungkook

BTS has mesmerized millions of fans globally with their unparalleled talent and irresistible charisma. With a diverse range of music that resonates deeply, they've shattered records, amassed countless awards, and continuously pushed creative boundaries. Their dynamic performances and innovative concepts have left an indelible mark on the music industry, captivating hearts across the world.
