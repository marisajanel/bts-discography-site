Wings/You Never Walk Alone

Wings released October 10, 2016

You Never Walk Alone released February 13, 2017

BTS's 'Wings' album marked a significant milestone in the group's career. This album dove into themes of temptation, desire, and the difficulties of youth. Following the success of 'Wings,' BTS released a repackaged version titled 'You Never Walk Alone.' The album includes tracks from 'Wings' along with new songs like 'Spring Day' and 'Not Today.' 'You Never Walk Alone' continues the narrative journey of 'Wings,' exploring themes of companionship, resilience, and the enduring bond between BTS and their fans, known as ARMY. Both albums received widespread acclaim for their musicality, storytelling, and emotional depth.
