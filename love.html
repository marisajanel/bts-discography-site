Love Yourself

'Her' released September 18, 2017

'Tear' released May 18, 2018

'Answer' released August 24, 2018

BTS's 'Love Yourself' album series, comprised of 'Love Yourself: Her,' 'Love Yourself: Tear,' and 'Love Yourself: Answer,' marked a significant phase in the group's musical journey. These albums explored themes of self-discovery, love, and acceptance. "Love Yourself: Her" introduced a vibrant and colorful sound with tracks like 'DNA' and 'Mic Drop,' setting the tone for the series. 'Love Yourself: Tear' dove deeper into the complexities of love and hearbreak, featuring emotionally charged songs like 'Fake Love' and 'The Truth Untold.' Finally, 'Love Yourself: Answer' concluded the series with a reflection on personal growth and self-empowerment, incorporating tracks like 'Idol' and 'Epiphany.' The Love Yourself albums not only showcased the musicality of BTS, but also their message of self-love which resonated deeply with BTS fans, ARMY.
